### **BrainMessenger Moderator Handbook**

**1. Your Mission & Core Principles**

* **Your Mission:** Your primary role is to cultivate and maintain a healthy, safe, and respectful environment within the BrainMessenger community. You are our community's "immune system," protecting users from "toxins" such as spam, harassment, and abuse.

* **Core Principles:**
    * **Fairness:** Make decisions based on the established rules, not on personal feelings or opinions.
    * **Accountability:** You are responsible for the integrity and safety of the community.
    * **Empathy:** Strive to understand all sides of a conflict before taking action.

**2. Your Responsibilities & Key Tasks**

* **Daily Tasks:**
    * **Review the Reports Queue:** Log in to the Admin Panel to review and process new user reports.
    * **Review User Feedback:** Monitor the feedback submitted to support@brain-messenger.com (or via the Admin Panel). Forward any significant ideas or critical feedback to me.

* **As-Needed Tasks:**
    * **Actioning Reports:** Based on our community guidelines, decide on the appropriate action for a report: ban the user, delete the content, or dismiss the report.
    * **Answering Basic Inquiries:** If a user contacts support with a simple question that is answered in the FAQ, politely direct them to the relevant section.

**3. Moderation Rules: Do's and Don'ts**

* **DO:**
    * **Ban users for:** Overt insults, spam, posting malicious links, hate speech, and fraudulent activity or scams.
    * **Delete content that:** Contains spam, harassment, illegal material, or other rule violations.
    * **Be courteous:** Always interact with users in a calm and professional manner, even if they are in the wrong.

* **DO NOT:**
    * **Ban based on personal dislike:** Never ban a user simply because you disagree with their opinion.
    * **Engage in lengthy arguments:** If a situation is complex or escalating, escalate it to the Administrator (me).
    * **Edit user messages:** Only delete messages that violate the rules. Do not alter them.
    * **Abuse your authority:** Do not use your moderator privileges for personal advantage or non-official purposes.

**4. Accountability & Consequences**

* **Importance:** Your work is integral to the reputation and health of our project. We entrust you with the responsibility of maintaining order and safety.

* **Warning:** Please be advised that any systematic violation of these moderation rules (e.g., unfair bans, consistently ignoring reports, abuse of power) will be addressed. The Administrator (Nikita Voitenko) may temporarily or permanently revoke your moderator privileges after an explanation is provided.
